What each page shows

The pages are built only from the dataset's published problems, solutions and validator. They show nothing beyond them.

  1. Objective and bounds

    Served service minutes, urgent minutes and travel time, next to the instance's upper bounds.

  2. Map

    Every job, each technician's start and end point, and the route each team drives, on OpenStreetMap.

  3. Timeline

    Each team's day: driving, service, waiting and the lunch break, against the shift.

  4. Jobs not served

    Every job left in the queue, with its time windows, priority and skills.
